What We Do

Music production, not generic AI music.

ROOM turns a prompt into a finished track, then gives producers the useful materials: full mix, stems, and MIDI. The workflow is designed to land inside real DAWs, not stay trapped in a browser.

ROOM is a Soloxholdings product built for producers, engineers, and artists who want generated material they can keep working with.
